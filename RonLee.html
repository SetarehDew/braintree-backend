Ron Lee

Ron Lee (1947–2017) was a beloved American artist and sculptor whose colorful, clown-inspired creations have brought smiles to collectors around the world. Known for his signature character “Hobo Joe,” Ron captured the humor, heart, and humanity of circus life in each hand-crafted piece. Starting his career as a young metalworker in California, he mastered the art of bronze and pewter casting, creating thousands of limited-edition sculptures over four decades. His work—often finished with hand-painted details and mounted on marble or onyx—blends craftsmanship, storytelling, and timeless charm. Even after his passing, Ron Lee’s joyful spirit continues to live on through every sculpture that finds a new home.
