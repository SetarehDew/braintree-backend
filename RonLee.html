Ron Lee
1947 – 2017
Creator of "Hobo Joe"

The Artist Behind the Smile

Ron Lee was a beloved American artist and sculptor whose colorful, clown-inspired creations have brought smiles to collectors around the world. Known for his signature character "Hobo Joe," Ron captured the humor, heart, and humanity of circus life in each hand-crafted piece.

Starting his career as a young metalworker in California, he mastered the art of bronze and pewter casting, creating thousands of limited-edition sculptures over four decades. His work — often finished with hand-painted details and mounted on marble or onyx — blends craftsmanship, storytelling, and timeless charm.

Ron Lee is a renowned artist whose work has captivated audiences with its unique blend of realism and fantasy. His art often features intricate details and vibrant colors, creating a sense of wonder and imagination. Lee's pieces are known for their emotional depth and storytelling, drawing viewers into the worlds he creates.

With a career spanning several decades, Ron Lee established himself as a significant figure in the art world, with his works exhibited in galleries and collections worldwide. Even after his passing, Ron Lee's joyful spirit continues to live on through every sculpture that finds a new home.
