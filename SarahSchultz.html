Sarah Schultz

In 1983, Sarah Schultz was managing the gift department in her husband’s pharmacy. During that time, she was already making and selling her own handmade creations. Over time, her craft-based designs evolved into the figurine/decorative business known as Sarah’s Attic. The figurines often feature children or animals and are hand-painted; for example, there is a figurine called “Percy Boy” that has a small painted heart on the pant leg, which is cited as a trademark of Sarah Schultz’s style. Sarah’s Attic figurines also appear in collectible/resale markets, suggesting they still have collector interest decades later.

Sarah Schultz is known for creating expressive, richly detailed artwork and collectible pieces with emotional depth, elegance, and powerful storytelling.

Her work brings together beauty, character, and meaning, making each piece a memorable addition for collectors who appreciate fine artistry.
