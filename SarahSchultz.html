About the Artist

Sarah Schultz is a gifted sculptor and painter whose work bridges two artistic disciplines with remarkable grace. Her pieces carry the warmth of handcrafted detail and the emotional resonance of fine painted artistry — making each one a truly one-of-a-kind collectible.

With a deep reverence for the human form and natural beauty, Sarah creates figurines and paintings that tell stories without words. Her work invites the viewer into quiet moments of reflection — a tender gesture, a thoughtful gaze, a scene frozen beautifully in time.

Sarah's dual mastery of sculpture and painting sets her apart in the world of fine art collectibles. Each three-dimensional piece she creates is finished with her signature painted detail — bringing texture, color, and life to every surface in a way few artists can achieve.

VJ Gallery is honored to feature Sarah Schultz's extraordinary work and looks forward to bringing her collection to collectors who appreciate artistry of the highest order.
